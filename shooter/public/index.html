THE RESIDENCE CHRONICLE · SPECIAL EDITION

A house with borrowed eyes.

A field guide for the two people foolish enough to enter.

First, disappear.

Connect two computers to the same network. Open the host’s server address, enter the same room code, and both select Ready. The host opens the doors.

The hider has 20 seconds to find cover while the hunter waits. Move with WASD, sprint with Shift, crouch with Ctrl. Listen for footsteps.

Then, borrow their sight.

After every 20 seconds of hunting, a 3 → 2 → 1 warning closes your vision. Both players exchange viewpoints for 7 seconds. Use the mouse to inspect their surroundings.

Both bodies freeze. No shooting or interactions. The round clock keeps running. When your own eyes return, movement and shooting resume. Five cycles make a 135-second hunt.

Finally, survive.

The hunter has six bullets: left click to shoot. Two body hits or one headshot ends the hunt. The hider wins by surviving or exhausting the hunter’s ammunition.

Press E at the bathroom portrait: hiders get one escape per player per match. E also flushes the toilet. Approach the bedroom cat quietly—or watch it flee.

Roles reverse in round two; both hunt in round three. Most round wins takes the match. In the last 10 seconds, hunters gain 10% speed and 15% light radius.

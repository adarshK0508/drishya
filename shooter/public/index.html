SIX THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU ENTER · A FIELD GUIDE FOR THE FOOLISH

First, disappear.

20 seconds to hide. WASD move · Shift sprint · Ctrl crouch. Listen: the hunter’s boots are heavier. Host a room, share its code; both Ready, then open the doors.

Whose eyes are these?

Every 20 seconds, a 3–2–1 warning precedes a 7-second sight exchange. Bodies freeze; mouse look stays free. Five cycles. Remember what you see.

Make each shot count.

Left click fires: 15 damage, 100 vitality. Roles reverse in round two; both get guns in round three. All three rounds count. Most round wins takes the match.

Take their eyes.

F, once in your hunting round: within 3m, same room, no wall. Choose their view for 3s; it locks for 3s. One separate 70-damage shot. Then 5s invisible escape, still vulnerable. Clock & swaps pause throughout. No special in round three.

A little more light.

One torch spawns each round. E to take it. Held in your left hand until round end: 15% farther sight, no beam. Its benefit follows the holder’s eyes, even during an exchange.

The walls have secrets.

E to investigate. The bathroom portrait offers a hider one escape per match. Some rooms have other surprises. Enter the master bedroom quietly… if you can.
